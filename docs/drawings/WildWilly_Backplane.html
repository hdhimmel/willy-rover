Why four layers

This rover is star-grounded by design (§10), and its two worst failures were ground failures. The GeeekPi breakout’s ground fault let sonar return current flow through the TRIG/ECHO lines and the Pi’s protection diodes — it floated the sensors’ reference, held ECHO high, and destroyed two HC-SR04s (§5.3, §16.12). A metal standoff under the MOSFET stack shorted the +12 V rail on 2026-09-28 and invalidated every rail reading taken near it. Today the star is a wiring convention — it holds only while whoever lands a ground wire remembers where it goes. On this board it becomes geometry.

Floorplan

Placement follows the grounding, and it lands on the same banding §2.2 already uses — which is a good sign rather than a coincidence. Quiet devices sit over the signal-ground pour, loud ones over the power-return pour, and the I²C trunk runs between them.

⚠ Q1 still protects only the battery input. Every rail downstream of it stays unprotected against a reversed connector, which is why D2 is per-Pico rather than one part. Four reverse-polarity events are on record: the encoder supply on 2026-09-18 (LF Phase A destroyed, all six Phase B greens dead since), two sonars (§16.12), and Pico B (2026-09-28).

And the rail boundary exists because of trimpots. R2 sat at 6.446 V on a nominal 5 V rail for eleven days — HC-SR04 absolute max is 5.5 V — and is the leading cause of the sonar lost in the 2026-09-28 smoke event. A future supply with fixed feedback resistors makes the rail a property of the board instead of a setting.

Open before layout

Five things this design cannot settle on paper. 1. Board outline and mounting — dictated by the deck, which is not dimensioned in any document. Measure first. 2. Connector family; JST-PH throughout today, and a keyed-per-function scheme needs choosing. 3. Fuse format — F2–F5 are ATC/ATO blade, which is large for a PCB, and a PCB-mount family changes the ratings available. 4. Whether the Pi mounts to this board or stays separate; separate today. 5. Cost at 4 layers for a deck-sized outline, unknown until the outline is measured.
