How to measure

  1. Lengths go between output-shaft centres, not horn edges or bracket ends.
  2. Willie on a flat floor, tyres at normal pressure: Hs and Hc depend on it.
  3. Angles: phone level app laid along the link, read at two pulses per joint. Two readings give degrees per µs and the zero pulse. Pick pulses far apart.
  4. θc: level the phone on the camera board, or aim at a floor mark of known distance and work it out from the frame.
  5. Gripper is already known: open 1150 µs = 73 mm, closed 2150 µs = 0.
